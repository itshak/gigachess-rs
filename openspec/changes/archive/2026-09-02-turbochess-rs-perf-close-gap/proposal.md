## Why

`turbochess-rs` after `turbochess-rs-perf-ultrachess-staged` improved perft **224→387 Mnps (+72%)** and `FEN write 155→77ns (−50%)`, `isCheck 2.5→0.47ns (−81%)`, `SAN 8.1→3.68µs (−54%)`, yet still trails `ultrachess` **836 Mnps (2.16×)** and `ultrachess/BENCH.md` micro targets (`movegen 65 vs 25ns`, `SAN 3.68 vs 1.43µs`, `make+unmake 1.08µs vs 503ns`, `isCheck/hash 0.47 vs 0.32/0.34ns`, `clone 430 vs 3.3ns`). The gap is not random: it is the **deliberate 4 losses** we kept (`Board 368B Copy + Chess960 rook squares` vs ultrachess `~100B`, no colour-templated movegen, no published magics, no incremental checker update) plus two remaining hot-path costs that `ultrachess` avoids: (a) **visitor pattern vs make/unmake+movelist** (2× per Gigantua) and (b) **template expansion for colour/EP/castling** (Gigantua's key win, see `Chess_Base.hpp: Lookup_Pext`). Users ask if the `2.1 Gnps` C++ projects (`Gigantua/Gigantua` 1.9 Gnps, Stockfish) can be copied to close the gap.
